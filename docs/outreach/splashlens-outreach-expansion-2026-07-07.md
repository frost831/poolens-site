## Deep small-company prospecting plan

Goal:

Build a verified queue of small-to-mid pool service companies, not a scraped blast list.

Recommended sources:

- Google Business Profile / Maps via approved API or manual exports, not ToS-breaking scraping.
- Company websites surfaced from local search queries.
- IPSSA, PHTA, chamber, BBB, state license, and local association directories where public contact details are intended for business contact.
- Manufacturer dealer/service locators where the terms allow outreach or manual verification.
- Podcast guest lists, local pool school attendee/sponsor pages, and trade-show exhibitor lists.

Pipeline:

1. Seed priority states: Florida, Texas, Arizona, California, Nevada, Georgia, South Carolina, North Carolina, Illinois, and Midwest markets with strong seasonal service demand.
2. Search city-by-city for public company websites and service pages.
3. Extract company, domain, public email/contact form, city, state, source URL, lane, and notes.
4. Dedupe against `splashlens-drip-queue.csv`, Gmail sent mail, replied rows, bounced rows, and suppressed rows.
5. Verify each row before sending.
6. Send small one-to-one batches only after the 7-day stop-signal rule is clear.
7. Keep the CTA practical: "Try the free app with one real code, part, or equipment family and tell us where it falls short."

Do not:

- BCC scraped lists.
- Email personal addresses from opaque data brokers.
- Ignore removal requests.
- Claim manufacturer endorsement, diagnosis, warranty, code compliance, or guaranteed fitment.
- Send to Fluidra support/product-support routes while suppressed.
