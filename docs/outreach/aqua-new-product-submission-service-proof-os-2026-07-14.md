## Product Name

SplashLens

## Product Category

Pool and spa service software / field reference and service proof app

## Short Description

SplashLens is a proof-first field app for pool and spa professionals. It combines PartSnap part-identification assistance, Service Proof Passport, Facility Assist, equipment/error-code lookup, dosing math, voice notes, and connected-pool troubleshooting so techs and operators can capture what happened before they order, escalate, or explain the next step.

## What Is New

SplashLens has expanded from a free field reference into a Service Proof OS for pool, spa, and aquatic service work.

The app now emphasizes:

- PartSnap for mystery parts, labels, codes, and equipment clues.
- Service Proof Passport for readings, photos, voice notes, customer-safe summaries, callback-risk flags, and next-visit context.
- Facility Assist for CPOs, swim schools, apartments, hotels, gyms, HOAs, and other operators who are responsible for a pool but are not repair techs.
- Connected Pool Network for robots, automation, lighting, salt systems, spa packs, heat pumps, covers, sensors, and chemical controllers.
- Owner usage visibility for real use signals such as first opens, scanner use, PartSnap results, proof saves, checkout success, and media/referral lift.

## Suggested News Write-Up

SplashLens, a free-core field reference app for pool and spa professionals, has expanded its workflow around proof-first service documentation.

The app includes PartSnap part-identification assistance, equipment and error-code lookup, dosing calculators, voice notes, connected-pool troubleshooting, and Service Proof Passport workflows. The goal is to help techs and operators document what they saw, what is still missing, and what needs verification before ordering parts, escalating to a senior tech or vendor, or sending a customer-facing update.

SplashLens also includes Facility Assist, a lighter workflow for CPOs, swim schools, apartments, hotels, gyms, HOAs, and aquatic facilities that may need daily checks, contamination-event documentation, basic equipment proof, and escalation packets without starting in a deep repair-tech workflow.

The app is free to try and does not require an account for core use. Optional paid workflows support heavier scanner/PartSnap usage. SplashLens is a reference and documentation tool; it does not replace manufacturer manuals, qualified technician judgment, CPO standards, health-department requirements, or manufacturer verification.
