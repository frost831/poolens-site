# PartSnap Partner Packet - SplashLens

## What PartSnap Is

PartSnap is the pool-part identification workflow inside SplashLens. A tech photographs a mystery part, model plate, molded number, or equipment label. The app returns possible matches, visible evidence, missing proof, alternative families, verification search terms, and a senior-tech/vendor escalation summary.

It is reference assistance only. It does not guarantee part fit, repair need, manufacturer approval, or diagnosis.

## Who It Helps

- Pool service techs trying to identify parts in the field.
- Service company owners training newer technicians.
- Pool schools and instructors building practical troubleshooting scenarios.
- Manufacturers and distributors who want fewer vague support requests.
- Creators and media who want a useful field-tech story.

## Partner Opportunities

1. Training scenarios
   - Provide real-world fault or part-identification examples.
   - SplashLens can turn them into conservative, field-safe training cards.

2. Parts verification
   - Help validate common part families, model plates, and visual clues.
   - Goal: fewer blind orders and cleaner escalation.

3. Sponsored education
   - Future banner or placement opportunities must be clearly disclosed.
   - No fake partner IDs, no implied endorsement, no hidden paid placement.

4. Feedback loop
   - Low-confidence PartSnap results can become training candidates.
   - Useful misses are more valuable than perfect demo screenshots.
