# SplashLens Spa / Hot Tub Lane Build - 2026-07-07

## What Changed

SplashLens now treats spa and hot-tub service as a first-class field lane instead of a small pool-adjacent note.

App-side lane now covers:

- Spa packs and controllers: Balboa, Gecko, Waterway NEO, topsides, heater tubes, sensors, flow/pressure switches, priming, high-limit, dry-heater, and GFCI trip workflows.
- Spa plumbing, jets, and pumps: weak jets, pump hum/no movement, air controls, diverters, filters, valves, and air locks.
- Spa water and sanitation: pipe/jet biofilm, sanitizer and pH drift, foam/odor, ozone, UV, AOP, cartridge/mineral systems, and small-volume dosing risk.
- Spa heat, swim spas, and covers: no heat, weak swim jets, waterlogged covers, and hospitality/high-use reset workflow.

Site-side lane now covers:

- `spa-hot-tub-troubleshooting-app.html` as the main public hub.
- `partsnap.html` and `partsnap-proof-library.html` with spa pack, spa plumbing, AOP/ozone/UV, and chemical-controller proof packets.
- `connected-pool-brain.html` with smart spa controller language and Balboa/Gecko/Waterway NEO discovery terms.
