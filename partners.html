Partner-Verified Cards

Give us the proof language. We turn it into field-safe cards.

The next SplashLens layer is a verified card network: manufacturers, distributors, educators, and expert vendors can provide model families, failure points, document links, aliases, and the words they want techs to use before ordering or escalating.

The ask: Give us your docs, known failure points, model families, and preferred verification language. We turn it into field-safe cards that help techs capture better proof before ordering, escalating, or calling support.
No fake verification. A card is only marked partner-verified after a real partner supplies and approves the language. Until then, SplashLens stays with possible matches, missing proof, and manual verification.

What we can ingest

  • Current install, service, or parts PDFs
  • Known failure points and common misidentifications
  • Model-family aliases used by techs and counter teams
  • Superseded part numbers and replacement notes
  • Training scenarios for CPO schools or service-company onboarding
  • Preferred safety and verification language
