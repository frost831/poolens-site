Field intelligence - July 16, 2026

What we learned from the current software and product field.

Adapt now

Finish-later field proof

Task Hub's draft-and-resume pattern is useful at the stop. SplashLens is adapting it for Service Proof Passport so techs can save incomplete proof locally, return later, and send a cleaner senior-tech packet.

Source: AQUA on Task Hub
Stay in our lane

Field intelligence beside the CRM

ProValet, PoolDial, Paythepoolman, and The Service Program are building routing, billing, scheduling, customer portals, and accounting. SplashLens should export better proof to those systems instead of rebuilding their back offices.

Source: AQUA on route automation
Connected equipment

CCEI / Vigipool remote-alert proof

Temperature, lighting, filtration, pumps, chemistry, remote monitoring, and alerts reinforce the Connected Pool Network lane. Techs need app screenshots, controller labels, manual readings, alert time, and local panel status before changing hardware.

Source: AQUA on Vigipool
Cover proof

Latham MeasureGO / MeasurePRO context

LiDAR pool-cover measuring is a manufacturer workflow, not ours to copy. SplashLens can still teach the pre-measure proof: device capability, pool perimeter, steps, raised walls, obstructions, anchor context, photos, and manufacturer verification.

Source: AQUA on MeasureGO
Cold plunge

Plunge All-In Gen 2 service clues

Faster cooling, ozone, higher-flow circulation, integrated temperature/pressure/flow sensing, stronger Wi-Fi, maintenance reminders, and remote control create a clear service-proof checklist for connected cold-plunge equipment.

Source: AQUA on All-In Gen 2
Proof export

Chemicals and supplies belong in the packet

Competitors use chemical and supply logs for billing. SplashLens should keep capturing product, amount, stock source, and estimated stop cost, then export that evidence without pretending to be the accounting system.

Source: AQUA on Active Invoicing
Dose provenance

Show the source behind the dose

In-app chemistry recommendations are useful only when the packet preserves the manual readings, pool volume, product strength, calculator or label source, suggested amount, and amount actually applied. SplashLens now adds Dose Basis to Service Proof drafts and exports.

Source: AQUA on integrated dosing
AOP proof

Jandy X-Series AOP service context

The new field card captures in-line or off-line model, pool volume, LED state, flow, plumbing orientation, mounting, lamp age, ozone tubing, check valve, and manual sanitizer reading. AOP remains supplemental treatment, not a replacement for required residuals or qualified service.

Source: AQUA on Jandy X-Series AOP
Safety proof

Smart pool cameras need a test record

SplashLens now covers camera fields of view, blind spots, armed state, walk tests, audible alarms, alert destinations, backup power, network state, keypad access, and privacy. Monitoring is supplemental and never replaces supervision, barriers, gates, covers, required alarms, lifeguards, or emergency plans.

Source: AQUA on SwamCam
Competitive boundary

Fast field flow, not another back office

Paythepoolman 2.0 reinforces the market demand for cleaner, faster mobile work. SplashLens is applying that lesson to save-and-resume proof, priority, and handoff while leaving scheduling, routing, billing, and invoicing to established service systems.

Source: AQUA on Paythepoolman 2.0
