What It Means

No Power to Unit

Likely Causes

  • GFCI outlet or breaker tripped (most common)
  • 240V supply not present
  • Disconnect switch off

Next Checks

  1. Check and reset GFCI protection at the unit or panel
  2. Check 240V breaker at main panel - confirm both legs are powered
  3. Verify all disconnect switches are in the ON position
  4. Check for 240V at unit disconnect with multimeter if available
