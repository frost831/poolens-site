Spa leak, winterizing, and rental reset checklist

Spa calls often become trust problems: a wet cabinet, freeze concern, heavy cover, guest turnover, or chemistry reset. SplashLens helps capture what was seen, what was done, what was declined, and what needs qualified review.

Capture first

1. water level trend2. leak only when pump runs?3. wet cabinet area4. equipment labels5. cover condition6. drain/refill history7. freeze risk8. customer approval/decline

Field-safe checks

  1. Separate splash, condensation, and wet covers from active plumbing leaks.
  2. Mark water level and document whether the leak appears with pumps off or only while running.
  3. Use the exact manual, company policy, and local climate practice for winterizing decisions.
  4. For rentals or high-use spas, capture readings, filter condition, bather-load context, and drain/purge history before reopening.
  5. Do not promise freeze protection, insurance coverage, or electrical/heater repair outcomes.
