Jandy / Zodiac JXi Gas Heater HIGH Certified tech recommended

What It Means

High Limit Switch

Likely Causes

  • Water temperature exceeded high limit (typically 135°F)
  • High limit switch failed
  • Same flow restriction causes as E02

Next Checks

  1. Allow full cooldown (20-30 min) before reset
  2. Verify water flow before restarting
  3. Test HLS switch resistance — should read 0Ω when cool; replace if open
