Field-safe checks

  1. Separate no-heat complaints from flow/filter restriction and owner modes.
  2. Record vibration, hum, or one-zone symptoms before opening anything.
  3. If the breaker trips, document when it trips instead of repeatedly resetting it.
  4. Leave energized component isolation to qualified service.
