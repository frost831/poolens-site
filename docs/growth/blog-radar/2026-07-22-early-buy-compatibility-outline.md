# SplashLens Blog Radar Outline

Date: 2026-07-22
Topic: Early-buy ordering, pump-rule timing, and retrofit compatibility proof

## Working title

Early Buy 2026: Why Pool Techs Need Better Compatibility Proof Before They Order

## Post goal

Translate recent industry signals about early-buy planning, pump compliance timing, discontinued models, and retrofit control products into a practical ordering checklist for pool and spa technicians.

## Structure

1. Lead
- The real 2026 shift is not only that companies are buying earlier.
- The harder shift is that pump, control, and replacement orders now require cleaner proof than many teams captured in the past.

2. What changed
- PoolPro shows early-buy planning now includes obsolete SKU reviews, warranty trend checks, and supplier conversations before orders are placed.
- DOE delayed enforcement penalties for small-size dedicated-purpose pool pump motors in a narrow future window, but the variable-speed direction remains.
- Recent product coverage shows more retrofit and control choices around the pump room, which raises compatibility stakes.

3. Why a pool or spa tech should care
- Wrong-part orders now waste more than money; they burn route time, callbacks, and customer trust.
- Discontinued models and converter-based retrofits make "looks close enough" ordering more dangerous.
- Teams that capture better proof can lock inventory earlier with less guesswork.

4. What proof to capture
- Full equipment pad photo
- Model and serial labels
- Voltage and total horsepower when readable
- Existing automation/control panel model
- Wiring and communication path
- Filter, heater, salt, and auxiliary gear connected to the same system
- Current symptom and customer goal

5. What to verify before ordering or repair
- Exact replacement path vs retrofit path
- Pump family, controller family, and accessory compatibility
- Whether the issue is really pump failure, bad programming, power, communication, or a chemistry side effect
- Whether the order is solving a recurring warranty problem or repeating it

6. Where SplashLens fits
- SplashLens is not the distributor, the pricing engine, or the compliance authority.
- It fits as the proof-first field layer that helps a tech hand off clean model, symptom, and context evidence before the office or supplier commits inventory.

7. Close
- The teams that win early-buy season will not just order sooner.
- They will order with better field proof.
