# SplashLens Mac / iOS Safari Visual Pass

Purpose: verify the premium homepage pass on real Safari before the next large marketing push.

## What Changed

- Homepage hero now leads with PartSnap and a real product screenshot.
- Hero CTAs are reduced to two field actions: Identify A Part and Prove The Visit.
- App Store and Google Play badges remain in the hero as supporting download actions.
- AQUA twice plus PoolPro coverage is now a compact proof strip in the hero and a tighter proof section lower on the page.
- Duplicate deep tool detail was removed from the homepage surface and left to tool pages.
- Motion is CSS-only, subtle, and content remains visible by default.

## Pass Checklist

1. Homepage first viewport shows the pool-pad photo, headline, two main CTAs, store badges, proof strip, and PartSnap screenshot without overlap.
2. No blank white sections appear while scrolling quickly.
3. Header does not cover hero CTAs or proof-strip links.
4. App Store and Google Play badges are sharp, aligned, and tappable.
5. The PartSnap screenshot is visible without stretching or cropping into unreadable noise.
6. Publication proof links open the AQUA Closing Season article, AQUA Field App article, and PoolPro launch article in new tabs.
7. Identify A Part opens the web app scanner path.
8. Prove The Visit opens the web app report path.
9. Mobile text is readable, no card/button text clips, and tap targets feel large enough with a thumb.
10. Reduced motion setting still leaves all content visible.

## Evidence To Capture

- One desktop Safari screenshot of the homepage first viewport.
- One iPhone Safari screenshot of the homepage first viewport.
- One iPhone Safari screenshot after scrolling to the PartSnap / proof area.
- Notes for any overlap, unreadable text, broken links, or slow/frozen scroll.

## Pass / Fail Rule

Pass only if the first viewport sells PartSnap clearly, no Safari-specific layout break appears, and the two hero actions land in the correct app workflows.
