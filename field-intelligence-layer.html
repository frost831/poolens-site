Where this goes without becoming a bloated CRM.

SplashLens should sit beside route, billing, and CRM systems first. The job is to own the field intelligence layer: proof, reference, training, escalation, and partner-ready equipment cards.

PartSnap Network

Second proof photo, mystery-part review tickets, partner-verified cards when available, and conservative fitment language.

Connected Equipment

Robots, automation, lighting, salt, heat pumps, chemical controllers, covers, spas, and swim spas organized into proof-first trees.

Training Product

New-tech mode, senior review, five-minute field lessons, CPO/facility flows, and partner training modules.

Business layers that can sit on top.

For service companies

Team proof history, branded customer summaries, callback-risk review, technician coaching, and weekly search/usage reports.

For partners

Manufacturer, distributor, training, media, and education partners can supply known failure points, docs, model families, and preferred verification language.

Important boundary: SplashLens is a reference and proof workflow. It should not claim diagnosis guarantees, official manufacturer approval, code authority, or replacement of qualified service.
