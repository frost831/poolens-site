What It Means

Motor Overtemperature

Likely Causes

  • Motor windings overheating
  • Blocked ventilation on motor housing
  • High ambient temperature in equipment area

Next Checks

  1. Check airflow around motor — minimum 6" clearance on all sides
  2. Remove any covers or enclosures blocking motor ventilation
  3. Verify ambient temperature is within motor operating range
  4. If motor runs hot even with adequate airflow, motor may need replacement
