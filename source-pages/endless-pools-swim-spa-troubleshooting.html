Swim spa source checklist

Endless Pools swim spa troubleshooting checklist

Endless Pools and fitness systems need model-specific proof around current behavior, controller state, filtration, maintenance status, and flow before a tech calls a pump, propulsion, or control failure.

Capture first

1. model family2. controller screen3. current setting4. water level5. filter condition6. propulsion/current proof7. suction/flow proof8. service history

Field-safe checks

  1. Separate weak-current complaints from dirty filters, water level, and user setting changes.
  2. Capture a short note about current surging, cavitation sound, or one-speed-only behavior.
  3. Verify whether the issue is swim current, heat, filtration, or control display.
  4. Escalate internal drive, control, and energized tests to qualified support.
