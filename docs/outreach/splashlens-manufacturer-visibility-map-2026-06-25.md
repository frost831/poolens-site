## Pitch guardrails

- Ask for accuracy feedback, one missing product family, or a practical training gap.
- Say SplashLens suggests possible matches and verification prompts; it does not diagnose, certify, guarantee fit, replace manuals, or imply manufacturer approval.
- Route any manufacturer co-branded idea through explicit written permission before using logos, badges, partner language, or verified-card language.
- For public media/creator asks, keep the best hook private to outreach copy unless we explicitly publish a challenge page later.

## Creative outreach lanes we were still missing

### Chemical companies and water-care educators

These are not just chemical vendors; they often publish training, dealer education, podcasts, webinars, troubleshooting guides, and water-balance content. The right ask is not "endorse SplashLens." The right ask is: "What chemistry references, calculator guardrails, or service-note prompts would keep a field tech from making a bad assumption?"

- HASA / Orenda: strong education lane around proactive chemistry, LSI, dosing judgment, and water-balance training. Pitch only as calculator/reference feedback; never imply SplashLens replaces label directions or calibrated testing.
- BioGuard / BioLab / KIK pool brands: dealer-training and chemical-line education lane. Good for training-pilot conversations and distributor/dealer feedback.
- Natural Chemistry / NC Brands, SeaKlear, SpaGuard, ProGuard, Pro Series: specialty chemical and dealer-education lane; useful for stain, scale, phosphate, clarifier, enzyme, and problem-water references.
- Lo-Chlor, Jack's Magic, Applied Biochemists, EasyCare, Robelle, GLB, In The Swim, Leslie's private-label education: add as chemistry-reference review targets after direct contacts are verified.
- Taylor Technologies, LaMotte, Palintest, WaterLink / Spin Touch routes: testing and readings lane. Ask about logging, proof photos, and "do not over-trust strip/camera readings" guardrails.

### Parts, accessory, and small-component companies

This is where PartSnap can become most useful because techs lose time on lids, baskets, gaskets, valves, unions, skimmer weirs, drains, light niches, transformers, tracks, brushes, cables, power supplies, and odd molded numbers.

- Waterway Plastics: pumps, filters, valves, skimmers, fittings, returns, drains, and accessory parts.
- Praher / Magic Plastics / valve and plumbing suppliers: diverter valves, check valves, unions, o-rings, actuator-adjacent plumbing clues.
- S.R.Smith, PAL Lighting, CMP Brilliant Wonders, J&J Electronics, Halco, Intermatic: lighting, niches, transformers, automation timers, retrofit clues, safety warnings.
- Pleatco, Unicel, Filbur, cartridge/filter-media suppliers: filter identification, cartridge cross-reference, sizing clues, and photo-proof prompts.
- AquaStar, Paramount, Waterway, CMP, Hayward fittings: drains, returns, VGB/safety-adjacent parts; keep language extra conservative.
- Skimmer basket, lid, o-ring, seal-kit, pump-motor, cleaner-track, cleaner-brush, cleaner-cable vendors: high-volume PartSnap edge cases and affiliate/partner-card candidates after permission.

- SCP / POOLCORP / Pool360: branch education, counter support, and distributor feedback.
- Heritage Pool Supply Group, HornerXpress / Team Horner, Pool Equipment & Supply, local independent distributors: counter packet and parts-counter validation lane.
- Pinch A Penny, Leslie's Pro, Namco, local retail/service hybrids: retail counter and service-team training lane.
- United Aqua Group and buying groups: member-benefit / vendor-demo / webinar route.
- IPSSA chapters, UPA chapters, regional service associations: chapter meeting, newsletter, vendor-night, and field-tool demo route.

## Suggested lane priorities

1. Distributor/counter lane: fastest feedback on PartSnap because they already solve mystery-part problems daily.
2. Chemical education lane: highest trust upside if calculator/chemistry language is reviewed conservatively.
3. Podcast/creator lane: best awareness lift if the ask stays practical and not hype-heavy.
4. Association/chapter lane: credible field adoption route with lower spam risk than random blasts.
5. Manufacturer product-support lane: slower but highest authority if any partner-verified card program develops later.
6. Builder/renovation lane: useful later for proof logs, warranty notes, and visual documentation, but not the top current wedge.

## Extra guardrails for these lanes

- Chemical lane: never say SplashLens tells a tech what chemical to add without label, volume, test-method, and qualified judgment verification.
- Safety/VGB/electrical/gas lane: avoid any shortcut language. Use "reference prompt" and "qualified verification" only.
- Manufacturer lane: no logos, official partner badges, verified cards, or co-branded language without written approval.
- Creator lane: do not publish private outreach hooks as public challenges unless we intentionally build a public campaign page.
- Distributor lane: ask for the counter's most annoying mystery-part category, not a generic testimonial.
