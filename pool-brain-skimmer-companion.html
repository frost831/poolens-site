Field-to-office handoff

1. Find the equipment clue

Use manual code lookup or an online scanner. Check the exact model, plate, and manufacturer manual before a repair or order.

2. Capture what is known

Record the symptom, photos, readings, possible match, and what still needs verification. Do not present a tentative part as confirmed fit.

3. Share the reviewed summary

Copy or use the device share sheet for the office, a senior tech, or a distributor counter, then attach or paste it into the existing job. SMS availability depends on the device and installed apps.

Bad signal? After an initial online load, cached manual lookup and calculators can work offline. PartSnap and AI scans need a connection. There is no claimed direct Pool Brain or Skimmer integration.

What to send to the counter

Bring the model plate, visible markings, dimensions, installed context, and the unanswered fit question. A concise proof packet gives the counter a better starting point; the manufacturer diagram and verified part number still decide the order.
