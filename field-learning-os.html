FAQ

Plain answers before anyone asks.

Is this replacing CPO training?

No. SplashLens can support scenarios and proof habits, but CPO training, local health code, facility policy, manufacturer manuals, and qualified service judgment still control.

Is this replacing Jobber, Skimmer, Pool Brain, or route software?

No. SplashLens is the field proof and learning layer. Route, billing, dispatch, and CRM tools can still run the company.

What can we ship first?

PartSnap lesson cards, Facility Assist scenarios, Service Proof review prompts, owner dashboard learning metrics, and partner intake for verified cards.

What requires a partner before we call it verified?

Manufacturer-approved cards, official training alignment, certification language, warranty-safe instructions, and any partner-branded lesson.
