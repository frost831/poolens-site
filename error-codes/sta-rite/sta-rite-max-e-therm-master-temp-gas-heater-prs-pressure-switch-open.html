Sta-Rite Sta-Rite MAX-E-THERM / MASTER TEMP Gas Heater HIGH

What It Means

Pressure Switch Open

Likely Causes

  • Insufficient water flow
  • Pressure switch failure

Next Checks

  1. Verify pump running and flow is adequate
  2. Clean or backwash filter
  3. Test pressure switch — professionals may jumper PS terminals briefly to confirm switch is the fault (do not leave jumpered)
