What It Means

Landscape / Strip Light Dim Or Flickering

Likely Causes

  • Voltage drop on low-voltage run
  • Undersized wire or transformer
  • Water in connector
  • Damaged strip segment

Next Checks

  1. Measure voltage at farthest fixture while lights are on
  2. Use proper low-voltage wire gauge and transformer tap
  3. Replace wet/corroded connectors
  4. Isolate strip sections to find damaged segment
