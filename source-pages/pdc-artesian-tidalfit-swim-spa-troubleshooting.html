Swim spa source checklist

PDC, Artesian, and TidalFit swim spa troubleshooting checklist

PDC, Artesian, and TidalFit systems need clean proof around controller family, swim current, jets, ozone, heat, and water quality before a tech orders pumps, topsides, or accessories.

Capture first

1. model and serial2. control screen3. active mode4. pump/current label5. jet bank behavior6. filter proof7. ozone status8. water readings

Field-safe checks

  1. Use the control guide for the exact pack/topside before interpreting icons or modes.
  2. Separate swim-current complaints from filter restriction, diverter settings, and water level.
  3. Check ozone and sanitizer proof before chasing persistent odor or foam.
  4. Escalate electrical, pack, drive, and heater testing.
