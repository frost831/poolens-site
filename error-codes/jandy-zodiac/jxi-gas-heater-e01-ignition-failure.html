Jandy / Zodiac E01: Ignition Failure

Jandy / Zodiac E01 reference for JXi Gas Heater: likely causes, next checks, and manual-verification reminders for pool service techs.

Jandy / Zodiac JXi Gas Heater HIGH Certified tech recommended

What It Means

Ignition Failure

Likely Causes

  • Gas supply off or too low
  • Faulty hot surface igniter (HSI)
  • Dirty flame sensor
  • Gas valve not opening
  • Flue backpressure (installation clearance issue)

Next Checks

  1. Verify gas shutoff valve is open
  2. Check manifold gas pressure (3.5" WC natural, 10" LP)
  3. Inspect HSI — should glow bright orange, replace if cracked or dim
  4. Clean flame rod with fine emery cloth
  5. Check flue clearance from walls — minimum per install manual
  6. JXi auto-retries 3x then locks out; hold MODE to reset lockout
