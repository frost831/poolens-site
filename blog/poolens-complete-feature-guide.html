Look Up a Code or Symptom

Search by displayed code, equipment family, or symptom, then open the relevant answer. Compare the actual model plate and current manufacturer manual before following a repair procedure. A general code-family answer is not proof that a particular part fits.

Use the Calculators

Enter measured water values and pool volume before reviewing a dose. Check units, product strength, label directions, and local requirements before adding chemicals. Record the reading and the action taken in the service platform your team already uses.

Try PartSnap as a Starting Point

PartSnap can suggest possible equipment or part families from a photo, but cannot guarantee an exact model or fit. Capture the whole unit, identification plate, close-up marking, dimensions, and installed context. Confirm the candidate against current manufacturer documentation before ordering.

Build a Proof Packet

Keep the code, readings, photos, observed condition, and verification status together so an owner, customer, distributor, or manufacturer can see what was checked. Copy or share the result using the options currently available in the app. SplashLens complements existing route, invoicing, and billing software; it does not replace those systems.

After an initial online load, cached manual lookup, calculators, guides, and checklists can work without signal. PartSnap and other AI scans require internet access. Browser storage may be lost if device data is cleared, so do not treat a local record as a guaranteed backup.
