Gecko spa pack troubleshooting checklist

Gecko packs and topsides need proof from the pack label, display, water movement, filters, and electrical boundary before a replacement path is trusted.

Proof to capture

1. pack label2. topside display code3. filter condition4. circ pump or jet pump proof5. heater/sensor area photo6. breaker or GFCI behavior

Field checks

  1. Separate low-flow symptoms from sensor/board assumptions.
  2. Check service mode and current owner settings.
  3. Escalate internal pack testing to a qualified tech.
Reference only. Verify current manuals, model numbers, dimensions, and qualified service boundaries before ordering parts or making repair decisions.
