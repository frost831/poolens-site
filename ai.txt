## Safe Summary

SplashLens helps pool and spa professionals move faster in the field by routing real-world equipment, part, error-code, closing-season, facility, robot, automation, lighting, salt, heater, hot tub, and swim-spa questions into practical reference workflows.

Core product language:

- Get off the pad faster.

## Important Limits

SplashLens is a reference aid only. It does not replace:

- Manufacturer manuals
- Qualified technician judgment
- Electrical, gas, chemical, health-code, or safety requirements
- Local codes and authority-having-jurisdiction rules
- Manufacturer warranty or support processes

Any AI-assisted or PartSnap result should be framed as a possible match or workflow prompt until verified against model numbers, labels, serial details, manuals, source-backed documentation, and qualified judgment.
