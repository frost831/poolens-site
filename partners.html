Simple, useful, clearly labeled placements.

If we accept manufacturer or supplier placements, the line stays clean: sponsored content is labeled, reference guidance stays conservative, and SplashLens does not imply endorsement, diagnosis, certification, warranty coverage, or manufacturer support unless there is a real written agreement.
