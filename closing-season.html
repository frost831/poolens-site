Midwest closing: the short answer

Close before sustained freezing conditions threaten water-filled equipment and lines; use your local NWS forecast, not a fixed city date. Test and document water, follow the cover and equipment manuals, isolate and drain what can trap water, winterize every identified line, and photograph plugs, valves, cover condition, and unfinished work. A qualified pro should handle uncertain plumbing or gas and electrical equipment.

Field checklist

The six closing lanes in the app.

Every lane is intentionally proof-first and manual-safe. The app helps remember what to capture, but the exact equipment manual, product label, local code, company policy, and qualified judgment stay in charge.

1. Pre-close proof and chemistry

  1. Record readings and water temperature.
  2. Save product names, strengths, and dose notes.
  3. Photo water clarity, pad, controller, and existing issues.

2. Equipment shutdown and drain proof

  1. Drain equipment that can trap water.
  2. Photo removed plugs, caps, unions, and storage location.
  3. Flag seized, missing, or questionable parts.

3. Lines, skimmers, returns, and antifreeze

  1. Document water-level decision.
  2. Record each line, skimmer, return, cleaner, spa, or water-feature loop.
  3. Use only pool-safe antifreeze where appropriate.

4. Heaters, salt, robots, and smart equipment

  1. Verify heater and heat-pump manual steps.
  2. Document salt cell status and robot storage.
  3. Capture automation/freeze-protection screens.

5. Cover and site closeout

  1. Photo anchors, straps, springs, bags, cables, pillows, or pump.
  2. Document cover-pump placement and discharge path.
  3. Leave customer-safe winter instructions.

6. Callback documentation

  1. Generate the customer-safe summary.
  2. Attach photos and open items.
  3. Save to history for spring opening.
