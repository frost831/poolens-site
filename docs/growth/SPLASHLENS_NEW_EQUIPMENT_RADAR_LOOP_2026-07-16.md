# SplashLens New Equipment Radar Loop

Purpose: keep SplashLens visibly current around pool, spa, swim spa, and facility equipment without making unsupported diagnosis, partnership, or fitment claims.

Weekly sources to review:
- AQUA Magazine product/news pages
- PoolPro and SpaRetailer product/news pages
- Manufacturer product pages and support libraries
- Distributor/new-product pages where public
- Trade show exhibitor/product announcements
- Public training/vendor education pages

Categories to check:
- Robots and cordless cleaners
- Automation, apps, panels, relays, actuators, Wi-Fi, RS-485
- Pumps, variable-speed pumps, heat pumps, chillers, gas heaters
- Salt systems, UV, ozone, AOP, ORP/pH/feed controllers
- Pool lights, transformers, niches, retrofit clues
- Spa and swim spa packs, topsides, current pumps, blowers, ozone, covers
- Safety/support tech such as cameras, covers, alarms, and facility logs

Update pattern:
1. Add a conservative field-safe card to the app only when the claim can be expressed as visible proof, likely category, known workflow, or manual-verification step.
2. Add or refresh the public SEO page only when it helps a real searcher: troubleshooting, parts identification, connected equipment, facility workflow, training, or proof.
3. Add the item to `new-tech-radar.html` or `whats-new.html` with no endorsement language.
4. Add a short outreach note only if the item creates a useful reason to contact a publication, trainer, manufacturer, distributor, or creator.
5. Track the source URL, date reviewed, claim boundary, and whether the app already has a matching card.

Claim boundaries:
- Say "possible match," "proof to capture," "verification step," or "field reference."
- Do not say "diagnoses," "guarantees fit," "official," "partner-verified," or "manufacturer-approved" unless there is written evidence.
- Safety tech is supplemental. It does not replace supervision, barriers, alarms, local code, or emergency plans.
- Chemical and dosing outputs require label, volume, strength, test method, and qualified judgment verification.

Outreach hook:
"We are keeping SplashLens current around new pool/spa equipment. If your team has preferred proof language, known failure points, model-family names, or support docs, we can turn them into field-safe cards for techs."

Success signals:
- New equipment page views
- App searches for the added category
- PartSnap proof packets tied to the category
- Feedback submissions naming the item
- Manufacturer/trainer replies with correction or preferred proof language
- Named pilot usage from service company, facility, or training partner
