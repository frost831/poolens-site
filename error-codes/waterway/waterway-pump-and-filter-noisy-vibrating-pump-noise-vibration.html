Waterway Waterway Pump & Filter MEDIUM

What It Means

Pump Noise / Vibration

Likely Causes

  • Pump not fully primed (air in system)
  • Impeller vane chipped or worn
  • Loose pump base mounting bolts
  • Motor bearing noise (early failure sign)

Next Checks

  1. Ensure pump is fully primed before diagnosing noise
  2. Remove lid and inspect impeller for chips or debris
  3. Tighten pump base mounting bolts
  4. If noise is a grinding or whining from motor: bearings may be failing, motor service needed

Verify Before Repair
