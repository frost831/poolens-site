Account and data requests

Use the verified account and data deletion form in SplashLens. Verify your email in the form and select the request that applies to you. Submitting a request is not confirmation that deletion is complete.

Read the SplashLens app privacy policy for details about device data, online scanning, account information, and retention.

Contact SplashLens support with questions about accessing, correcting, or exporting your data. For deletion, use the verified request form.
