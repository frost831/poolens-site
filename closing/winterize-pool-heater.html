Winterize a pool heater

Turning a heater off does not remove trapped water. Identify the make, model, and fuel or power type; have a qualified technician follow its shutdown, isolation, and drain procedure. Photograph each specified drain point after drainage and note removed parts and valve positions. Gas, electrical, and pressure work must follow the exact manual and local requirements.

Document the heater close

  1. Record heater make, model, and fuel or power type.
  2. Have a qualified technician follow the model's shutdown and isolation steps.
  3. Photograph each model-specified water drain point after drainage.
  4. Record removed parts, valve positions, and spring restart cautions.

Raypak's public winterizing guidance describes shutting off gas and power, isolating water where necessary, and draining the heater and other equipment. It also notes that some heater designs have multiple drain locations. This is not a universal procedure for heat pumps, electric heaters, or every Raypak model. A model manual and qualified technician decide the actual sequence.

Escalate uncertainty

Do not loosen an unknown fitting to search for a drain point. If the heater cannot be identified, isolated, or drained as specified, record the limitation and escalate. A photograph of an off switch is not proof that the water side is empty.

Proof to capture

  • Nameplate and wide photo of piping, valves, and service access.
  • Shutdown and isolation evidence from the qualified technician.
  • Each drained header, plug, or model-specific module.
  • Removed-parts storage and the opening-season restart note.

Questions

Is turning off a pool heater enough for winter?

No. Water can remain in the heater after it is switched off. The installed model's manual controls shutdown, isolation, drainage, and spring restart.

Can the same drain-plug instructions be used for every heater?

No. Drain points differ by heater model and may be on multiple headers or modules. Identify the exact heater and have a qualified professional follow its manual.
