Spa brand proof checklist

Nordic, Viking, Dynasty, Saratoga, and Adirondack spa troubleshooting checklist

These brands can use common control families, but the service path still depends on model year, pack label, topside family, and manual route. Capture the proof before ordering a topside, pack, filter, heater, cover, or leak part.

Capture first

1. brand badge2. cabinet serial/model3. spa pack label4. topside panel5. display code6. filter dimensions7. cover condition8. symptom timing

Field-safe checks

  1. Separate a setting or lock mode from a failed controller before quoting parts.
  2. Match the current owner/manual route before relying on an old cross-reference.
  3. Use filter measurements and end-cap style for cartridge proof; do not order by brand name alone.
  4. Keep electrical, heater, GFCI, and internal pack diagnostics inside qualified service boundaries.
