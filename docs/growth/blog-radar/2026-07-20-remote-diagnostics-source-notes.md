## Draft angle

Working title:
- Remote Pool Diagnostics Is Becoming a Real Workflow. Techs Still Need Proof on the Pad

Core thesis:
- In 2026, pool industry software and equipment vendors are pushing the same direction: more remote visibility, more connected planning, and more traceable operating data.
- That does not remove the need for on-site verification.
- The winning workflow is remote signal first, field proof second.

## Required field-proof checklist to emphasize in the post

- Equipment photo with current screen state
- Model and serial confirmation
- Controller or automation status at the pad
- Live water test reading, not app history alone
- Network or offline status when connected features are involved
- Before/after photo if a part change, chemical correction, or alert reset happens
- Manual verification before ordering parts or escalating a warranty claim

## Guardrails

- Do not say SplashLens replaces Skimmer, Pool Brain, Pool360, Pentair automation, or Datapool.
- Do not say connected equipment eliminates service visits.
- Do not repeat vendor efficiency claims as universal truth.
- Keep SplashLens framed as field reference plus proof capture support before office software, parts ordering, or customer communication.
