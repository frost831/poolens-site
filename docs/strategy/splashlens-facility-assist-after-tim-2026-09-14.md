## What Tim Originally Validated

The useful signal was not a custom Aquatic Council build. It was the lighter operator workflow:

- Many CPOs, facility managers, apartment operators, swim-school staff, maintenance people, and aquatics directors are responsible for the pool but are not deep repair techs.
- They need the first safe action, documentation, dosing support, code/standard routing, and escalation, not a full technical flowchart.
- Common support categories include contamination events, daily readings, chemical dosing, pump/motor basics, codes/standards, facility logs, and when to call qualified help.
- Call logs from training/support programs can become a ranked roadmap if a partner is willing to share them under NDA or in scrubbed form.

## What We Should Not Do

- Do not chase Tim as the only route.
- Do not present Facility Assist as Aquatic Council-backed.
- Do not build a full CRM or certification product from this lane.
- Do not claim reopening decisions, code compliance, warranty authority, insurance coverage, or health-department approval.
- Do not copy CPO course material or manuals.

## The Lane To Pursue

Facility Assist should stay a partner-led access product inside SplashLens:

**Responsible operator -> short wizard -> proof packet -> qualified escalation or documented closeout.**

It sits on top of existing SplashLens assets:

- Facility mode in the app.
- Daily check, dose, contamination, pump/basic equipment, manual/label, and support lanes.
- Service proof workflow.
- Closing Season Mode.
- Field Learning OS.
- Owner/usage dashboard.
- Tracked links and QR front doors.

## Best Buyer Targets Now

1. CPO/AFO training providers who want post-class reinforcement.
2. Swim schools and aquatic programs with distributed staff.
3. Apartment/HOA pool operators.
4. YMCA, park district, municipal, campground, hotel, and university aquatic operators.
5. Pool service companies that sell facility support retainers.
6. Manufacturer or distributor support teams that want better pre-call proof.

## Product Package

Working name:

**Facility Assist Pilot**

Plain-English offer:

Give your operators a simple front door for daily checks, contamination events, dosing questions, basic equipment proof, and support packets.

Pilot structure:

- 14 days.
- 3 to 5 facilities or operator users.
- One tracked QR/link per facility.
- One support route.
- One weekly summary: opens, lanes used, packets created, repeated issues, missing workflow requests.
- End with a review call and a keep/change/kill decision.
