What It Means

Chlorine Output Below Setpoint

Likely Causes

  • Cell approaching end of life (production decreases over time)
  • Salt level slightly low
  • CYA too high (masks FC reading — pool may be adequately chlorinated)
  • Setpoint too low for bather load or pool size

Next Checks

  1. Run cell inspection diagnostic — compare cell amps to expected output for cell size
  2. Increase output setpoint by 10% and monitor FC
  3. Test true FC with Taylor K-2006 or FAS-DPD — strip tests are unreliable for SWG pools
  4. Replace cell if amps consistently below 1A on cell diagnostic
