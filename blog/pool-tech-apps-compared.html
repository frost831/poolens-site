Best Apps for Pool Technicians 2026: Full Comparison

Date: November 15, 2025Pool ProTime: 7 min read

The pool service tech in 2026 runs a mobile business. Between route management, chemical logging, customer communication, and field reference, the right app stack can save hours per week - or cost you them if you pick the wrong tools. This comparison covers every major category: route software, chemistry tools, and field reference, with honest pricing and real assessments.

Category 1: Route Management and Business Operations

Skimmer (check current plan)

Skimmer is a pool-specific route management platform for scheduling, service logging, customer communication, invoicing, and team operations. Its public pricing page lists plans from $49 and $98 per month with per-location terms. Whether it pays for itself depends on the work your route actually saves.

Pool Brain (request a current quote)

Pool Brain is another pool-specific platform for route and business operations. Compare a current vendor quote and a hands-on trial against the same locations, users, and workflows; this article does not assume it is cheaper or simpler.

Jobber (check current plan)

Jobber is a general field service management platform rather than a pool-specific reference. Compare its current scheduling, billing, and field-data workflow with the pool-specific options before choosing a plan.

Service Autopilot (check current plan)

SA is a powerful but complex platform used by larger pool service companies. It includes marketing automation, HR tools, and advanced reporting. Significant setup time required. Best for: companies with 5+ technicians and dedicated admin staff.

Category 2: Chemistry Tools and Field Reference

SplashLens (Free)

SplashLens focuses on quick field reference, dosing calculators, and proof of a service stop. Manual lookup, calculators, guides, and checklists are free and can work from cache after an initial online load. Online AI scans require a connection and a free field profile; optional Pro adds unlimited scans and saved job memory.

SplashLens is a stop-level companion, not a substitute for scheduling or billing. Compare the current chemistry features of any route platform directly before deciding which tools you need together.

Pool Math by TFP

Pool Math is a well-regarded chemistry calculator from the TroubleFreePool community. The free version handles basic calculations. The paid tier adds logging, chemical tracking, and test history. It's built around the FC-CYA relationship central to the TFP methodology, which is excellent chemistry. Best for: techs who follow the TFP approach to sanitization. Complement with SplashLens for broader field reference.

Orenda App (Free)

Orenda Technologies' app calculates the Langelier Saturation Index (LSI) in real time as you enter test readings. The LSI is essential for preventing plaster etching and calcium scale. The app is excellent for chemistry-forward operations. Best for: commercial techs and anyone managing plaster or pebble-finish pools who cares about balanced water, not just compliant chlorine levels.

Category 3: Communication and Documentation

Google Photos / iCloud (Free)

Every tech should photograph equipment problems, chemical issues, and anything that could become a dispute. Your phone's native photo app, geotagged and timestamped, is your best documentation tool and it's free. Sync to cloud automatically.

Text Message (Free)

The most effective customer communication tool is still a direct text with a service photo. Skimmer automates this; solo operators without route software can send a weekly service photo via text and achieve the same retention effect.
