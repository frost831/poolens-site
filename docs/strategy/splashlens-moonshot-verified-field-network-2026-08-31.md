# SplashLens Moonshot - Verified Field Network

Date: 2026-08-31

## North Star

SplashLens helps pool and spa pros get off the pad faster with better proof, fewer guesses, and cleaner handoffs.

## Moonshot

SplashLens becomes the verified field network for pool, spa, and connected backyard equipment work.

The free app gets a tech unstuck. The network layer turns real field moments into reusable intelligence:

- What part, code, symptom, or equipment family showed up.
- What proof was visible.
- What proof was missing.
- What got sent to a boss, supplier, counter, trainer, facility, or manufacturer.
- What became a callback, repeat question, training need, or partner card.

## The Strategic Bet

Do not fight full CRM platforms head-on. Route, billing, scheduling, invoicing, and broad customer portals are already crowded.

Own the moment before the CRM record is useful:

- The weird part in the tech's hand.
- The code on the screen.
- The automation/app issue nobody wants to guess at.
- The closing-season proof that saves a later argument.
- The facility/CPO event that needs documented escalation.
- The parts-counter call that should have had one more photo first.

## Core Product Loops

1. Tech loop
   - Open SplashLens.
   - Search, scan, or start a workflow.
   - Capture proof.
   - Mark Helpful, Wrong, Missing, or Saved me time.

2. Counter loop
   - Before a part order, SplashLens asks for model plate, old-part markings, second photo, symptom, code, and fit clues.
   - Output becomes a cleaner counter/vendor packet.

3. Partner loop
   - Manufacturer, distributor, trainer, or expert partner supplies docs, model-family aliases, known failure points, and preferred verification wording.
   - SplashLens converts that into field-safe cards after real review.

4. Learning loop
   - PartSnap misses, support packets, facility incidents, and proof gaps become five-minute field lessons.
   - Owners see where techs are getting stuck.

5. Intelligence loop
   - Dashboard tracks first opens, searches, scans, missing searches, saved proof, feedback, return use, checkout starts, and paid conversion.
   - Product roadmap is driven by actual field friction instead of guesses.

## Pricing Shape

- Free Core: lookup, calculators, basic PartSnap, notes, and field reference.
- PartSnap Pro: heavier scanner use and faster field workflow.
- Solo Proof: saved job trails, customer-safe notes, and proof packets.
- Teams: owner dashboard, repeat misses, saved proof, coaching signals, and field reports.
- Facilities: daily checks, event workflows, support packets, and staff handoffs, shaped with real training/operator partners.
- Partner Cards: manufacturer, distributor, trainer, or support workflows after real review.

## What Would Make This Defensible

- A growing field corpus of parts, codes, symptoms, model-family aliases, and proof requirements.
- Real feedback loops after every scan/search/proof packet.
- Named field testers and permission-based case studies.
- Partner-approved cards that competitors cannot simply scrape.
- Source-backed PartSnap candidates separated from AI-only suggestions.
- Owner dashboard evidence that proves time saved, avoided callbacks, and training gaps.

## Seven-Day Proof Target

- 100 tracked campaign visits.
- 30 app opens.
- 15 completed field workflows.
- 10 direct feedback responses.
- 5 returning users.
- 1-3 paid conversions.
- 3 usable field stories.

## What To Stop Doing

- Do not become a generic CRM before the field layer is winning.
- Do not imply manufacturer verification without written partner approval.
- Do not sell AI magic. Sell a faster, safer field workflow.
- Do not count QA traffic as product traction.
- Do not ship unsupported public claims such as inflated database counts.
