What It Means

Water Entry Alarm

Likely Causes

  • Water has entered the electronics compartment
  • Seal or gasket damaged or displaced
  • Robot dropped or impacted before water entry

Next Checks

  1. Remove robot from pool immediately
  2. Do NOT plug in or charge until fully dry
  3. Allow robot to air-dry completely (24-48 hours minimum)
  4. Inspect all seals, gaskets, and housing for cracks or damage
  5. Contact Betta/BWT support — electronics water exposure may require professional service
