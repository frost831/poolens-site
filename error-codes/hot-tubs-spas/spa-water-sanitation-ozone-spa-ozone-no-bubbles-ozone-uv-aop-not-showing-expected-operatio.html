What It Means

Ozone / UV / AOP Not Showing Expected Operation

Likely Causes

  • Injector not pulling air
  • Check valve failed
  • Ozone tubing blocked or wet
  • UV lamp aged out
  • Flow too low through treatment loop

Next Checks

  1. Check for visible air draw only when the system should be active
  2. Replace wet or failed check valves before water reaches the unit
  3. Capture module label, tubing route, and flow context
  4. Follow lamp replacement intervals for UV/AOP
  5. Do not open energized ozone/UV equipment without qualified service
