What It Means

Low Or Uneven Water Feature Flow

Likely Causes

  • Feature valve partly closed
  • Dirty filter or low pump speed
  • Nozzle clogged
  • Air leak on suction side
  • Multiple features not balanced

Next Checks

  1. Open/adjust feature valve and increase pump RPM
  2. Clean filter and pump basket
  3. Remove and flush nozzles or laminar screens
  4. Balance each feature valve one at a time
  5. Check for air in pump basket indicating suction leak

Verify Before Repair
