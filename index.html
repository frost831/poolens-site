How It Works

Zero setup. Open it and go.

SplashLens is live on the iOS App Store and Google Play, and the free web app still works from the browser on any device.

1

Open app.splashlens.com in your browser

On any phone, tablet, or laptop. No download, no login. Just open the URL and you're in.

2

Add to your home screen (optional, takes 5 seconds)

Install from the App Store or Google Play, or open the web app from any browser. The browser route still supports a full-screen install option when available.

3

Save your pools once

Enter each pool on your route - name, volume, shape, filter type. They're saved to your device. Every subsequent visit, just pick the pool and the calculators pre-fill.

4

Use it offline all day

After the first load, SplashLens caches everything to your device. Pull the error code reference at a garage panel with no signal. Run the SLAM calculator in a basement equipment room. It just works.

Questions

FAQ

Is SplashLens really free? What's the catch?

Core SplashLens tools are free forever: manual lookup, calculators, checklists, notes, and reference guides. PartSnap Pro is optional at $4.99/month or $39/year for techs who need extended online scanner access.

How does offline mode work?

When you first open SplashLens, the service worker caches the app, error code data, calculators, and reference tables to your device. Manual lookup and calculators work without signal after first load. AI camera scanning requires internet.

Does SplashLens compete with route apps or service CRMs?

No. Those are service management platforms: billing, invoicing, customer CRM, route optimization. They run the company. SplashLens supports the stop with code lookup, scanner, dosing, reports, and poolside reference. We intentionally do not do billing.

How do I install it on my phone?

On iPhone, install SplashLens from the App Store. On Android, use the Google Play listing or open app.splashlens.com and use the browser install option when available. The web app remains free for field reference use.

What brands are covered in the error code library?

Hayward (TriStar, Super Pump, MasterTemp, OmniLogic), Pentair (IntelliFlow, IntelliFlo, MasterTemp, EasyTouch), Jandy (iAqualink, JXi), Zodiac, Maytronics/Dolphin (robot cleaners), Polaris, Kreepy Krauly, Waterway, and more. Beatbot and Betta robotic cleaners are in the current expansion.

What does the AI scanner actually do?

Error Scan, PartSnap, and Test Strip reader are live online. Error Scan attempts to read pool equipment displays and returns reference causes plus next checks. PartSnap suggests possible part IDs and search terms. Test Strip reader estimates readings for quick field triage. Without internet, use manual lookup and manual entry.

Is my pool data private?

Pool profiles, chemistry history, and route data live on your device in localStorage. AI scan images are sent to the scanner endpoint only when you use the online AI feature. There is no account or backend sync for your customer list.
