# SplashLens North Star Execution

Date: 2026-09-01

## North Star

SplashLens is the field intelligence and job-proof layer for pool, spa, swim-spa, and facility work.

The operating promise:

1. Identify the thing.
2. Prove the visit.
3. Send the right note.
4. Learn from the miss.
5. Verify with real partners when available.

SplashLens should not become a bulky CRM. CRMs handle scheduling, invoices, and customer records. SplashLens should make the field stop cleaner before the tech, owner, counter, trainer, or facility contact has to make the next call.

## Conversion Priority

Visibility is not the bottleneck by itself anymore. The current bottleneck is activation proof:

- Did a real person open the app?
- Did they complete one useful workflow?
- Did they tell us whether it helped, was wrong, or was missing information?
- Did they return?
- Did they hit checkout or ask for a pilot?

The owner stats dashboard now needs to be read as a funnel, not a pile of event counts:

1. Article / site traffic
2. App or store intent
3. First field action
4. Useful result
5. Feedback captured
6. Return / continued use
7. Checkout intent
8. Paid / entitlement proof

## 7-Day Operating Target

Target one measurable week, not generic growth:

- 100 tracked campaign visits
- 30 app opens or store clicks
- 15 completed field workflows
- 10 direct feedback taps or notes
- 5 returning users
- 3 named pilot conversations
- 1-3 paid conversions or serious paid-pilot asks

## First 60 Seconds

The first job is to get the user into one obvious task:

- Service tech: PartSnap, code lookup, dosing, job note.
- Facility / CPO: daily check, contamination event, dose note, equipment acting up, help packet.
- Counter / distributor: part, model, proof, missing evidence before ordering.
- Trainer: real stop to five-minute lesson.
- Homeowner: plain note, basic math, when to call a pro.

The main public CTA should remain:

Try one real code, part, or equipment family.

## Paid Wedge

Keep the free core free:

- Manual lookup
- Basic PartSnap
- Calculators
- Basic Facility Assist
- Reference pages

Sell only when the workflow saves or organizes work:

- PartSnap Pro: heavier online scanning.
- Saved Job Pro: saved history, customer notes, proof, repeat issue watch.
- Team Field View: owner visibility, searched/missed reports, company-branded job notes.
- Facility / CPO pilot: daily checks, contamination notes, staff handoff, help packets.
- Partner-verified cards: only after real partner review.

## Named Pilot System

Use the existing 14-day pilot package, but push it harder:

- 25 individual techs
- 5 service companies
- 3 facility/training pilots

Each pilot gets a tracked link with a `DEMO-*` or named pilot id until they consent to real identity storage. Do not turn a click into a story. Do not turn a story into a public quote without explicit permission.

## Closing Season Push

The strongest seasonal angle for September is:

Close clean now so spring does not start with mystery notes, missing plug photos, wrong part assumptions, or customer disputes.

Best target lanes:

- Midwest service companies
- Pool schools and CPO instructors
- Distributor counters
- Heater, cover, salt, pump, and robot suppliers
- Publications, podcasts, and field creators
- Facility operators with seasonal shutdown responsibilities

## Content Loop

Publish or prepare one small useful update at least three times per week:

- Closing proof checklist
- Heater or pump proof prompt
- Robot or automation radar note
- Spa/swim-spa winterizing proof note
- One PartSnap miss and what proof was missing
- Facility/CPO scenario
- Five-minute field lesson

Every content item should point back to either the app, the field challenge, or a partner/facility intake route.

## What Not To Claim

Do not claim:

- Diagnosis
- Guaranteed part fit
- Warranty approval
- Manufacturer endorsement
- Insurance protection
- Partner verification unless approved in writing
- Install/download counts unless proven from store or analytics evidence

Use:

- Possible match
- Proof prompt
- Source-backed where applicable
- Manual and qualified verification required
- Partner-verified only after real review
