Polaris (Zodiac) Polaris Pressure-Side Cleaners MEDIUM

What It Means

Booster Pump Off or Drive Train Issue

Likely Causes

  • Booster pump not running (for 280/380/480 models)
  • Feed hose kinked or disconnected
  • Drive belts broken or missing
  • Turbine/water wheel clogged
  • Water flow to cleaner too low

Next Checks

  1. Verify booster pump is running and circuit breaker is on
  2. Check all hose connections at wall fitting and cleaner
  3. Inspect all three drive belts — replace if cracked or broken
  4. Remove turbine cover and clear debris from water wheel
  5. Verify booster pump valve is open fully

Verify Before Repair
