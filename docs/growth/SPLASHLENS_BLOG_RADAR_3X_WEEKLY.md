# SplashLens Blog Radar - 3x Weekly

Purpose: turn current pool, spa, hot tub, equipment, software, training, and service-industry news into original SplashLens blog drafts.

Cadence: Monday, Wednesday, Friday.

Primary source lanes:
- AQUA Magazine: products, business software, service, retail, news, and New & Improved roundups.
- PoolPro Magazine: service, business, training, current issue, and product/news coverage.
- Service Industry News: service technician, chemistry, safety, repair, and regulation topics.
- Pool & Spa News: builder/service business, M&A, education, regulation, and manufacturer/product updates.
- Pool Magazine: pool service, equipment, business, automation, and industry commentary.
- Manufacturer newsrooms and product pages: Pentair, Hayward, Fluidra/Jandy/Zodiac/Polaris, Maytronics/Dolphin, Raypak, AquaCal, Aiper, Beatbot, Waterway, Balboa, Gecko, CCEI, Latham, and related equipment companies.
- PHTA/IPSSA/regulatory/training sources when a topic affects field workflow, safety, CPO operations, or technician education.

Rules:
- Do not copy or rewrite source articles. Use source links as research inputs, then write original SplashLens analysis.
- Keep quotes short and rare. Prefer paraphrase, field implications, and verification checklists.
- Cite and link every source used.
- Do not claim SplashLens is endorsed by a publication, manufacturer, trainer, or partner unless there is explicit signed approval.
- Keep claims conservative: SplashLens is a field reference aid, not a diagnosis, compatibility, safety, code, warranty, or manufacturer-manual replacement.
- Every post should answer: what changed, why a pool/spa tech should care, what proof to capture, what to verify before ordering/repair, and where SplashLens fits.

Best recurring post formats:
- New Tech Radar Brief: 3-5 new products or product updates, each translated into field proof prompts.
- Field Workflow Takeaway: one industry software/product article, with what SplashLens should adopt or avoid.
- Equipment Watch: one category such as robots, automation, lights, salt, heat pumps, AOP, covers, cold plunge, spa packs, or swim spas.
- Training/CPO Brief: safety, standards, contamination events, dosing logs, facility documentation, or operator workflows.
- Competitive Boundary Note: explain why SplashLens stays field-reference/proof-first instead of becoming a full CRM.

Output expectation:
- Save source notes and draft outlines under `docs/growth/blog-radar/`.
- If confidence is high and the topic is clearly original, create one publish-ready HTML draft under `blog/` using the existing blog style.
- Update `blog/index.html` only when the post is ready for publication and has been locally checked.
- Run link/copy checks, commit scoped changes, and report the draft/post path plus source links.
