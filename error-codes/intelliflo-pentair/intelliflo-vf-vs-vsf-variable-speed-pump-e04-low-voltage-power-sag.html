What It Means

Low Voltage / Power Sag

Likely Causes

  • Supply voltage below nameplate rating
  • Undersized breaker or wiring
  • Extension cords in use

Next Checks

  1. Check supply voltage at motor terminals (should be 240V ±10%)
  2. Verify breaker amperage matches motor requirements
  3. Extension cords are not permitted — run direct wiring to motor
