What app identifies pool parts?

SplashLens includes PartSnap, a field reference workflow for pool service techs who need possible part-family clues without pretending a photo is enough to guarantee fitment.

Try PartSnapHow PartSnap Works

Short answer

SplashLens PartSnap can help identify possible pool part families from a photo, visible markings, model plates, labels, dimensions, and surrounding equipment context. It is built for technicians, so it also asks what proof is missing before anyone orders a part.

What PartSnap looks for

Visible markings

Molded numbers, labels, model plates, amperage plates, serial clues, logos, unions, ports, and casting marks.

Equipment context

Pump, filter, salt system, heater, robot, light, valve, automation panel, cover, feeder, or controller family.

Proof gaps

What photo, measurement, or manual check is still needed before confident ordering or escalation.

Do not order from a photo alone. Use PartSnap as a starting point, then verify model, diagram, dimensions, and manufacturer documentation.
