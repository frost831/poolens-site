Field promise

No blind replacement claims.

PartSnap is a reference workflow, not a diagnosis, warranty, or fit guarantee. It is designed to slow down the dangerous part of guessing and speed up the useful part: proof, notes, escalation, and verification.

  • Possible matches, not final answers
  • Manual and qualified tech verification still required
  • No manufacturer partnership implied unless clearly marked
  • Sponsored placements must be disclosed
What to capture

Better photos make better field packets.

PartSnap works best when the tech captures both the part and the proof around it. A clean close-up helps identify markings, while a second wider photo helps show where the part lives on the equipment.

Before ordering, verify the possible match against the model plate, current parts diagram, dimensions, and qualified tech or manufacturer guidance.
Useful proof photos
  • Old heater labels and control boards
  • Salt cell markings and flow switches
  • Automation boards, relays, actuators, and remotes
  • Light niches, transformers, and retrofit clues
  • Robot parts, power supplies, tracks, brushes, and cables
  • Valves, unions, lids, baskets, seals, sensors, and O-rings
