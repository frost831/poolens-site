## Direct answer

What evidence helps before ordering an unidentified pool part? Keep the exact equipment model and variant, an accessible label, visible markings, an exterior overview and dimensions available without disassembly together with the applicable manufacturer parts reference. Mark missing fields as unknown. A visual candidate or AI confidence label does not establish compatibility. Ask the manufacturer or supplier to confirm the proposed replacement against the exact equipment before ordering. See the worked documentation example and open PartSnap: https://splashlens.com/partsnap-proof-library.html#before-ordering .

## Supplier handoff checklist

Before a pool-parts order, check your handoff: exact equipment model and variant; readable accessible label; exterior overview; visible part marking; available dimensions; current manual revision and parts-list section; unresolved fit questions; and the person obtaining missing evidence. Keep candidate matches separate from manufacturer or supplier confirmation. Hold the order while identity or compatibility is unresolved. Do not open equipment or expose wiring just to complete this documentation checklist; use qualified service when obtaining a detail requires that work. Example request: https://splashlens.com/partsnap-proof-library.html#supplier-handoff .
