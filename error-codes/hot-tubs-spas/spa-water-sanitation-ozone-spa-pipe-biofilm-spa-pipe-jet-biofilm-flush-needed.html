What It Means

Spa Pipe / Jet Biofilm Flush Needed

Likely Causes

  • Biofilm in plumbing
  • High bather load
  • Long drain interval
  • Sanitizer kept too low
  • Foam/odor after jets run

Next Checks

  1. Use a spa pipe and jet purge product before draining when biofilm is suspected
  2. Run jets per product label, then drain and rinse thoroughly
  3. Clean filters separately
  4. Refill and balance water before sanitizer startup
