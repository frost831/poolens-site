Fast workflow

Capture the label. Spa pack, pump, heater, sanitizer cartridge, ozone unit, or display code.
Verify water manually. Do not let a cartridge, app, or guess replace a water test.
Check flow first. Filters, valves, air locks, and jet restrictions explain many no-heat and weak-jet calls.
Separate safe from qualified. Electrical heater checks, sealed packs, and GFCI issues need qualified service.
Save proof. Store photo, symptoms, checks, and customer-safe summary before leaving.
