# SplashLens Field Clips - Two-Week Script Pack

Date prepared: 2026-07-23

Format: vertical 9:16, 20-35 seconds, captions burned in, real app capture, one claim and one action per clip.

Global content gate:

- Use a real demonstration item or clearly label the item `DEMO / TEST`.
- Never describe a possible match as confirmed fitment or diagnosis.
- Do not show customer names, addresses, serial numbers, gate codes, invoices, or private service records.
- Verify the featured workflow on the current production build before recording.
- Use `SplashLens is a reference aid. Verify against the model, manual, manufacturer guidance, and qualified judgment.` in the caption or end frame.
- Do not publish a time-saved, callback, accuracy, partnership, or customer claim without retained evidence and permission.
