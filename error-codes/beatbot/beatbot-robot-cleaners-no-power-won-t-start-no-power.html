Beatbot Beatbot Robot Cleaners HIGH

What It Means

No Power

Likely Causes

  • GFCI outlet tripped
  • Power supply failed
  • Float cable damaged
  • Charging dock fault
  • Battery fully depleted (not charged before first use)

Next Checks

  1. Press reset button on GFCI outlet and test outlet with another device
  2. Inspect float cable for cuts or pinches
  3. Verify power supply output with multimeter if available
  4. Charge robot fully before first use (4+ hours)
  5. Try a different outlet on a different circuit

Verify Before Repair
