What It Means

Compressor Overcurrent

Likely Causes

  • Power supply amperage issue
  • Compressor hard-starting
  • Start capacitor failed

Next Checks

  1. Check power supply voltage and amperage at unit disconnect
  2. Test start/run capacitor — replace if out of spec
  3. If capacitor is good and compressor still hard-starts, contact HVAC/pool tech for compressor service

Verify Before Repair
