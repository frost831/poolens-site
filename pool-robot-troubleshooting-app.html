What should I record before asking for robot-cleaner support?

Before asking for robot-cleaner support, record the cleaner model and serial label, its separate power supply or charger label, the exact indicator or app message and observation time, accessible exterior photos, and the applicable manufacturer manual. Mark unreadable details as unknown. A photo, library entry or PartSnap candidate does not confirm a failed component or replacement fit. Do not open a battery or electrical enclosure to obtain evidence.

Three separate evidence records

Cleaner identity

Record the full label and variant as seen, an exterior overview and visible markings. Put “unknown” beside an unreadable field. A family name is not the complete installed-model identity.

Power supply or charger

Keep its accessible label and visible connector separate from the cleaner identity. Record the displayed state without treating a light or photo as proof that the cleaner, battery or supply has failed.

Symptom and time

Write the exact display/app text or light pattern, when it was observed and what changed beforehand. If an app is involved, record its version and the relevant screen; leave customer and account details out.

Review before sending or ordering

  • Separate observed facts, reported symptoms and inferred candidates.
  • Mark missing model, marking, time or measurement fields as unknown.
  • Keep the exact source document and section beside the question.
  • Request manufacturer or supplier confirmation while identity or fit is unresolved.
  • Review the intended recipient and remove customer identities, access codes and account information before sharing.

A support note does not authorize repair, establish warranty coverage, confirm compatibility or replace qualified service. Battery, electrical and sealed-equipment concerns belong with the manufacturer or an appropriately qualified person.
