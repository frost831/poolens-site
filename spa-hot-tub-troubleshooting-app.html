Pool + spa field reference

Hot tubs and swim spas belong in the same field workflow.

Many service companies touch both pools and spas. SplashLens adds a spa lane for the fast checks: weak jets, no heat, sanitizer drift, pipe biofilm, filters, spa packs, ozone systems, and customer-safe proof notes.

First spa lanes

Flow

Weak jets and pump air locks

Filter condition, air controls, jet inserts, slice valves, pump prime, and proof photos before quoting parts.

Heat

No-heat checks

Flow switch clues, mode/setpoint, dirty filters, display codes, breaker behavior, and qualified electrical boundaries.

Water

Sanitizer and pH drift

Small-volume chemistry moves fast. Track test proof, cartridge/feeder state, oxidation, TDS age, and drain/refill timing.

Purge

Pipe and jet biofilm

Foam, odor, long drain intervals, and jet-line buildup get a purge-before-drain workflow and proof note.

Systems

Spa packs and ozone

Capture spa pack label, display code, sensor clues, ozone status, and whether the issue is plumbing, control, or electrical.

Proof

Customer-safe summaries

Turn findings into a clear note: what was checked, what is likely, what still needs manual/model verification, and what is unsafe to guess.

Fast workflow

Capture the label. Spa pack, pump, heater, sanitizer cartridge, ozone unit, or display code.
Verify water manually. Do not let a cartridge, app, or guess replace a water test.
Check flow first. Filters, valves, air locks, and jet restrictions explain many no-heat and weak-jet calls.
Separate safe from qualified. Electrical heater checks, sealed packs, and GFCI issues need qualified service.
Save proof. Store photo, symptoms, checks, and customer-safe summary before leaving.
Reference only. Spa equipment varies heavily by manufacturer and model. Verify all service steps against current manuals, labels, water tests, and qualified judgment.
