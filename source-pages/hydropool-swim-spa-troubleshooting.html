Hydropool swim spa troubleshooting checklist

Hydropool swim spa calls often overlap routine maintenance, water level, filtration, pump behavior, heat, and control settings. Capture proof before deciding whether it is a part, setting, or service issue.

Capture first

1. model label2. topside screen3. water level4. filter proof5. pump labels6. swim jet behavior7. manual water test8. maintenance history

Field-safe checks

  1. Rule out water level and filter restriction before pump or heater assumptions.
  2. Compare swim jet behavior at each setting and note any cavitation or surging.
  3. Document water care and filter maintenance because high-use swim spas drift quickly.
  4. Hold electrical, pack, and heater work for qualified service.
