Date: April 23, 2026SplashLensTime: 5 min read

What a Tech Can Use Now

Manual code and symptom lookup, dosing calculators, guides, checklists, and visit notes are available in the web app. Manual tools remain free. The app supports a proof-first stop: collect the displayed code, equipment label, readings, photos, and what still needs checking before a part is ordered or a customer summary is sent.

PartSnap and Online Scans

PartSnap can suggest possible equipment or part families from a photo. It is not an exact part or fitment guarantee. Capture the full equipment view, identification plate, close-up marking, dimensions, and installed context; confirm against the manufacturer documentation before ordering. Online AI scans need a connection and a free field profile. Optional Pro access adds unlimited scans and saved job memory.

Offline Boundaries

After a first online load, cached manual lookup, calculators, guides, and checklists can work without signal. PartSnap and other AI scans require internet access. Local browser records can be lost if the browser data is cleared; do not treat local storage as a guaranteed office backup.

Where SplashLens Fits

SplashLens helps with the field stop and its proof packet. It does not replace scheduling, invoicing, or billing software. Use your existing service platform for those jobs and copy or share the verified field details where they belong.
