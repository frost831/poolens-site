What It Means

Cover Will Not Open Or Close

Likely Causes

  • Key switch/controller not sending power
  • Breaker/GFCI tripped
  • Motor thermal overload
  • Cover track obstructed
  • Mechanism jammed

Next Checks

  1. Check breaker/GFCI and controller power
  2. Remove leaves/debris from tracks and box
  3. Let motor cool if it stopped mid-cycle
  4. Do not force a jammed cover; inspect ropes, pulleys, and mechanism
  5. Keep people out of pool area while troubleshooting

Verify Before Repair

SplashLens is a field reference, not a repair guarantee. Confirm the visible code, exact model, and current manufacturer procedure before replacing parts, bypassing safeties, or quoting a customer.
