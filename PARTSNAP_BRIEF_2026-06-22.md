# PartSnap Brief

PartSnap is the SplashLens pool-part identification workflow. It is meant for the moment a tech is standing at the pad with a mystery part, label, impeller, seal, grid, salt cell, robot component, valve, light, fitting, basket, gauge, O-ring, controller, or pump-pad hardware and needs a faster path to a useful answer.

The important positioning is this: PartSnap is not just image search. The moat is the workflow around the image:

- photo of the part
- prompt for the model plate, molded number, serial label, dimensions, or equipment family
- possible manufacturer/model/part-number clues
- missing-proof checklist
- confidence language that avoids fake certainty
- useful search terms
- escalation packet for a senior tech, distributor counter, vendor, trainer, or manufacturer

The strongest product promise is not "we identify everything." The stronger and safer promise is "we help the tech gather cleaner proof before ordering, calling, or escalating."

Best public wording:

> PartSnap turns a mystery pool-part photo into possible manufacturer clues, model-proof prompts, missing evidence, and a cleaner escalation packet before anyone orders parts.

What it can support:

- pump impellers, seals, lids, baskets, motors, unions, and O-rings
- filter grids, cartridges, manifolds, gauges, clamps, and air relief parts
- heaters, heat pumps, labels, sensors, and control boards
- salt cells, feeders, unions, flow switches, and controllers
- automation panels, relays, actuators, and control labels
- pool lights, transformers, niches, lenses, and GFCI symptoms
- robot tracks, baskets, cables, power supplies, and wearable parts
- covers, water features, valves, bubblers, and deck jets
- SCP/Pool360 counter searches and distributor escalation packets

What it should not claim:

- guaranteed diagnosis
- guaranteed part fitment
- official manufacturer catalog coverage
- endorsement by brands, distributors, or chemical lines
- replacement for manuals, calibrated tests, qualified electrical/gas work, or manufacturer documentation

Immediate feature wishlist:

1. Require a second proof photo for model plate or label when confidence is not high.
2. Save PartSnap result into Service Proof Passport.
3. One-tap copy/share packet for senior tech, distributor, vendor, or customer-safe internal notes.
4. Mystery Part Lab for low-confidence submissions that become training examples.
5. Partner-verified cards for manufacturers, distributors, instructors, and chemical educators.
6. Callback Risk Score based on weak proof, unsafe conditions, unverified fitment, or missing model context.
7. Apprentice Mode where the app asks what the new tech would check before revealing the answer path.
