What SplashLens Needs

Model families

Common names, aliases, generations, labels, serial clues, and what techs call the product in the field.

Proof requirements

Photos, label angles, serial/model plates, readings, visible symptoms, measurements, or tests required before confident routing.

Known failure points

Common symptoms and the safe first checks a qualified tech should perform before parts, escalation, or support calls.

Preferred language

Words to use and words to avoid around diagnosis, fitment, warranty, safety, certification, chemical treatment, or support routing.

What We Will Not Claim Without Approval

No fake partner labels

A card is not marked partner-verified unless a real partner supplies and approves the language.

No diagnosis guarantee

SplashLens stays a reference aid. It does not replace manuals, labels, tests, codes, licensing, or qualified judgment.
